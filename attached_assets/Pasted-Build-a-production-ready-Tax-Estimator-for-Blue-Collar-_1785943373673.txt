Build a production-ready Tax Estimator for Blue Collar AI.

The estimator must use existing scanned paystub data and also allow full manual entry.

Core calculations:
- Estimated annual gross income
- Regular wages
- Overtime wages
- Double-time wages
- Bonuses
- Standby/on-call pay
- Taxable per diem versus non-taxable per diem
- Federal taxable income
- Federal income tax estimate
- Social Security tax
- Medicare tax
- Additional Medicare tax when applicable
- Oklahoma state income tax
- Estimated total annual tax liability
- Taxes already withheld
- Expected refund or amount owed
- Effective tax rate
- Marginal tax bracket
- Remaining estimated tax per paycheck

Support:
- Weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, and monthly pay schedules
- Variable work hours
- Storm weeks and irregular overtime
- W-2 and 1099 income
- Filing status
- Dependents
- Standard deduction
- Pre-tax 401(k)
- Roth 401(k)
- Traditional IRA
- HSA contributions
- Health insurance deductions
- Other pre-tax payroll deductions
- Multiple jobs
- Spouse income
- Federal and Oklahoma withholding
- Year-to-date earnings and withholding

Build two calculation modes:

1. Quick Estimate
Uses the latest paystub and annualizes current earnings.

2. Detailed Estimate
Uses year-to-date figures, projected remaining work, contributions, deductions, filing status, dependents, and additional income.

Create scenario comparison:
- Current contribution rate
- Increased 401(k) contribution
- Maximum 401(k)
- With and without overtime
- W-2 only versus W-2 plus 1099 income

Display:
- Estimated refund or amount owed prominently
- Calculation breakdown
- Monthly and annual figures
- Confidence level based on data completeness
- Missing-information warnings
- Tax-saving opportunities
- Disclaimer that estimates are informational and not professional tax advice

Accuracy requirements:
- Store tax-year-specific federal and Oklahoma tax rules separately
- Never hard-code tax rules directly inside UI components
- Create a versioned tax rules/calculation module
- Make future tax-year updates easy
- Use exact progressive tax-bracket calculations
- Prevent double-counting paystub records
- Distinguish taxable wages from gross cash received
- Do not treat qualifying non-taxable per diem as taxable income
- Do not assume deductions that the user did not enter

Integration:
- Read existing paystub data from PostgreSQL
- Allow users to select which paystubs and income sources are included
- Save tax scenarios to the user account
- Add a Tax Estimator navigation item
- Add a tax summary card to the enhanced dashboard after its task is merged
- Follow existing Clerk authentication, Drizzle ORM, audit logging, rate limiting, and design system patterns

Testing:
- Add unit tests for tax brackets, payroll taxes, deductions, irregular overtime, multiple jobs, and refund/amount-owed calculations
- Add edge-case tests for incomplete paystubs, duplicate paystubs, negative values, unusually high overtime, and mixed W-2/1099 income
- Run typecheck and build before completion
